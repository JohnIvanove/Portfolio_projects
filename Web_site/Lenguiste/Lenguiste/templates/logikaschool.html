• Lenguist

Information card Lenguist
Main objective:
  • Lenguist aims to teach languages (English and Ukrainian) through the creation of language cards by users. Users can create their own word and phrase cards for a more personalized learning experience.
Interface and Design:
  • Lenguist has a user-friendly and intuitive interface with simple forms for creating and editing cards. The main emphasis is on functionality and user-friendliness.
Content:
  • Content is generated by users through the creation of language cards. This allows you to have a large amount of diverse content that adapts to the needs of each user.

Lenguist is a platform for learning languages which you create.

  • — Functionality:
  • Registration and Authorization: Users can register, log in, and manage their accounts.
  • Card Creation: Users can create cards with language pairs (English-Ukrainian).
  • Learning: Users can use their cards for learning by choosing different modes (quizzes, true/false, input answers).
  • Security: IP addresses and browser information are stored to improve security and analysis.

• Logika school

Information card Logika school
Main objective:
  • Logika School English is aimed at teaching English to children. They offer interactive courses, video tutorials, and playful learning methods.
Interface and Design:
  • Logika School English has a professional and modern design with a focus on visual appeal. The interface is adapted for easy access to training materials and courses.
Content:
  • The content is developed by experts in the field of English. The courses are structured and aimed at achieving specific learning objectives.

Logika School is aimed at professional language learning.

  • — Functionality
  • Registration and Authorization: Users can register and access various courses.
  • Online Courses: Users can take various English language courses.
  • Video Lessons: Access to video lessons and materials that can be watched at the user's convenience.
  • Forum and Support: Users can discuss questions in forums and receive support from instructors.
— Complete:

Both sites provide unique opportunities for learning and development. Lenguist offers personalized language learning through user-generated language cards, while Logika School focuses on structured online courses in a variety of disciplines.

If you're looking for a platform to learn programming, math, or other disciplines, feel free to visit Logika School English and check out their courses and materials. They can be a great addition to your educational journey!
