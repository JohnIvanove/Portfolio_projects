Introduction

Welcome to Lenguist! We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal information. This privacy policy describes how we collect, use, disclose, and protect your information.

Information Collection

We collect the following types of information:

  • Personal Data: Name, email address, login details.
  • Technical Data: IP address, browser type, access time.
  • Card Data: Words and phrases created by users for language learning.
Use of Information

We use your information for the following purposes:

  • Improving our services and user experience.
  • Storing and managing your language cards.
  • Providing customer support.
Disclosure of Information

We may disclose your information to:

  • Partners and service providers who assist us in providing services.
  • Government authorities, if required by law.
Information Protection

We use various technical and organizational measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, use, or disclosure.

User Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal information.
  • Correct or update your data.
  • Delete your data from our databases.
Cookies

We use cookies to enhance the functionality of our website and provide a personalized experience.

Changes to the Privacy Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new version on our website.
